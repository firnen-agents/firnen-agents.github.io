Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Creator Analytics is a private tool used by one creator to analyze their own content. It has no public users.

What it accesses

  • With the creator's permission, read-only data from their own YouTube and TikTok accounts: profile details, video details and performance metrics.
  • Public comments on the creator's YouTube videos, including commenters' display names.

How it is used

The data is used only to show the creator how their content performs and to suggest tags and video ideas. Video details and performance data may be sent to Anthropic's Claude to generate those suggestions.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored in a private database, with sign-in tokens encrypted, and in encrypted backups. It is not sold, and it is not shared except with the service providers needed to run the tool.

Removing access

The creator can revoke access at any time from their Google or TikTok account settings. Stored data can be deleted on request.

Contact

Open an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
